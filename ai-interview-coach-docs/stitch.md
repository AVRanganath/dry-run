# Google Stitch UI Design Prompt

Copy and paste the prompt below into Google Stitch to generate the UI components for the AI Mock Interview Coach. When you export the code as a ZIP, you can feed it back to Antigravity for implementation.

---

**Prompt for Stitch:**

Build a comprehensive Next.js web application UI for a premium, professional mock interview coaching platform. 

**Design Aesthetic (CRITICAL):**
I specifically DO NOT want the typical "AI aesthetic" (no glowing purple/blue gradients, no sparkles, no dark space themes). Instead, the UI must have a highly tactile, physical, and textured feel with significant depth. It should look like a high-end, modern analog office or a bespoke physical dossier.
* **Textures & Depth:** Use subtle paper grain, noise overlays, inner shadows, and embossed/debossed elements. Elements should feel like they have physical weight (e.g., using neomorphism strictly for subtle tactile buttons or cards, layered on distinct elevations).
* **Colors:** Rich, earthy, grounded tones. Think cream/off-white paper backgrounds (`#F4F1EA`), deep forest greens (`#1A3626`), rich terracotta or burnt orange accents (`#C85A32`), and deep charcoal for text. 
* **Typography:** Use a sophisticated Serif font (like Playfair Display, Merriweather, or a modern serif) for primary headings and a highly legible, utilitarian Sans-Serif (like Inter or Roboto Mono) for data, code, and small UI text. It should feel editorial and premium.

**Pages & Views Required:**

1. **Dashboard (Progress Hub):**
   * A tactile, card-based layout.
   * A prominent "Start New Interview" button that looks like a physical, heavy switch or raised button.
   * "Performance over time" visualized not as a standard digital chart, but styled like a physical ledger or a highly stylized analog gauge/dial showing their average rubric score.
   * A list of "Recent Sessions" resembling a stack of physical case files (subtle overlapping shadows).

2. **Setup View (The Briefcase):**
   * A split view or stepped form.
   * Left/Top: "Job Description" input area styling that resembles a clipboard or a textured document pad.
   * Right/Bottom: "Resume" upload/paste area. 
   * The inputs shouldn't just be flat borders; give them an inset, debossed look so they feel carved into the page.

3. **Active Interview View (The Interrogation Room):**
   * Very focused, distraction-free UI.
   * The current question displayed prominently in a beautiful serif font on a raised "card" in the center.
   * Below it, the answer input area. It should have a toggle between "Type Answer" and "Record Audio". If "Record", show a tactile, mechanical-looking record button (deep red indent) and a realistic audio waveform visualizer.
   * A progress indicator showing "Question 2 of 5" styled like mechanical tally marks or physical dots.

4. **Feedback & Rubric View (The Report Card):**
   * Displayed after an answer is submitted or at the end of the interview.
   * Needs to look like an official, graded exam paper or dossier.
   * Show the score (1-5) prominently, perhaps utilizing a "stamped" visual effect.
   * Sections for "Strengths" and "Areas to Improve", utilizing rich iconography and distinct, textured pill tags.
   * A breakdown of the rubric (Clarity, Relevance, STAR method) with visual progress bars that look like physical sliders.

Make sure to include rich micro-interactions (hover states that push down like physical buttons) and ensure the entire app feels cohesive, mature, and remarkably unique compared to standard SaaS dashboards.
